Doctor: Good morning. Please tell me what happened.
Patient: Good morning doctor. I fell from my bike this morning, and my right leg is badly broken. The pain is terrible.
Doctor: I am sorry to hear that. Can you move the leg?
Patient: No, I cannot move it. The lower leg looks bent and it is swelling very fast.
Doctor: Did you hit your head when you fell?
Patient: Yes, a little. I fainted for a few seconds right after the fall. And there was heavy bleeding from a cut on my leg, but a friend tied a cloth around it.
Doctor: Okay. Can you feel your toes? Are they cold or numb?
Patient: They feel numb, and my foot looks pale. I have also been feeling very thirsty since the accident.
Doctor: Alright. Let me examine the leg carefully.
Patient: Please doctor, will I need surgery? I am very scared.
